# Ten Rules for Ten Years · script

Series: **The Guardian & the girl** (two characters only, from `assets/ref/keyart.png`).
- **G** = the Wise Guardian, a faceless hooded AI mentor who lives inside the ancient clock.
- **L** = the little girl.

The answer is a countdown from ten to one. It is written to differ from two reference advice lists the user supplied (not included): none of their items appear, reworded or otherwise.

Retention devices:
- **The question is the hook,** on screen from frame 0.
- **An open loop by 12 %:** "the last one… almost nobody follows".
- **A visible countdown,** so every viewer knows how much is left and waits for number one.
- **A re-hook at about 45 %:** the girl asks whether number one is the secret.
- **A pattern break before the payoff:** the clock stops, then 1 s of silence.
- **A payoff at about 80 %** that turns the Guardian's own setting (the clock) into the lesson.
- **A two-word ending and a loop-friendly last frame:** the girl looking up, smiling, like frame 0.
- **A comment prompt:** "Which rule are you starting today?"
