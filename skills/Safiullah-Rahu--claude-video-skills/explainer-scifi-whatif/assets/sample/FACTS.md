# One Year Away · FACTS

The story is fiction: the company (DEEPARC), the courier job, the ship that reaches 99.99 % of light speed, OTTO, Mia and
the update log are invented. The science the story leans on is real; each claim below is checked against two sources
(checked 9 Nov 2026).

| # | claim (line) | value used | sources |
