Notes
- The 1973 ban is stated in the past tense on purpose (a 2025 executive order asked the FAA to revisit it; the sample
  makes no claim about the rule's status today).
- Visual liberties, all labelled: the aircraft shape and livery are approximations; the shock-wave shells and ground
  rings visualise pressure waves (illustration); towns and microphones are illustrations ("Illustration · communities
  not shown"); the F-5E, first-flight and first-supersonic scenes are recreations. The chase plane is drawn as a
  generic twin-tail fighter; the comparison airliner is a generic twin turboprop. Channel mark "Vectorwing" is original.
