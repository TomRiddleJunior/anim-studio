## YouTube Shorts — description
In 1846 a treaty split the American and British West along the 49th parallel, and the line left the tip of one
peninsula on the American side. Point Roberts, Washington, can only be reached by land through Canada. Its school
runs to third grade; after that, children ride about 40 minutes through British Columbia to Blaine, crossing the
border four times a day. Since a 1987 agreement, its water has been bought from Greater Vancouver.
