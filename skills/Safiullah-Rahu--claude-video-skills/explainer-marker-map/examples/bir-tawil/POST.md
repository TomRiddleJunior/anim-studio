## YouTube Shorts — description
Bir Tawil is 2,060 km² of desert on the Egypt–Sudan border with no permanent settlements and no coast. The 1899 agreement put the border on the 22nd parallel. In 1902 Britain drew a separate administrative boundary that followed how the land was used. Egypt keeps the 1899 line, which puts the Halaib Triangle (20,580 km², on the Red Sea) in Egypt. Sudan keeps the 1902 line, which puts Halaib in Sudan. Neither claims Bir Tawil. Sudan withdrew its forces from Halaib in January 2000, and Egypt has administered it since.
