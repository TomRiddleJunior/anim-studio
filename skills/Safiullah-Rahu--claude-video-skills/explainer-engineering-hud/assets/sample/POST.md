## Instagram / TikTok caption
In December 2023 a storm pushed the North Sea toward Rotterdam, and the city's sea gate closed itself. A computer
called BOS reads the water forecasts; when Rotterdam is expected to reach 3 m above sea level, it starts the closing
on its own. Two hollow 210 m gates swing out on ball joints and sink to the riverbed in about two hours. That night
all six of the Netherlands' big storm barriers were shut.

Sources: Rijkswaterstaat, Wikipedia, Schuttevaer, Water News Europe. Photo inset: Michielverbeek, CC BY-SA 3.0.
