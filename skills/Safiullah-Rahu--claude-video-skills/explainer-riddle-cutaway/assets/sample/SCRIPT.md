## Retention devices
- **Frame 1:** the counter pill, a person on the stairs and the first pill; the first word lands at 0.21 s.
- **The observer's "?"** at 18 s invites the viewer to solve it.
- **The anomaly** is framed with "something strange happens"; the boring answers are ruled out at 31–33 s.
- **The countdown** is a real 10 s (one click per second), with "Comment your answer" on the card.
- **The reveal** is shown twice: in the x-ray view, then in close-ups of the device (ring, "Hello?", click).
- **The limit tag** "Two quiet minutes" is the quotable line; the laugh releases the tension.
- **The ending** loops into frame 1.
- **The clue is fair:** when 306 is empty there is no snoring and no call (the card shows exactly that).
