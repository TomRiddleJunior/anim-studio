## Fair-clue design
- She jolts in every setup shot (O1, O1b, O6, O7), silently. The anomaly evening ("she leaves looking worse") still jolts.
- The card shows the difference: the pans get a smile and a "Thank you!", the water doesn't.
- The hic sound is heard only in the replay, so the setup doesn't give the answer away by ear.
