# Born Second, Born First — script and shot list (riddle-twins)

- **Format:** `explainer-riddle-cutaway`. The third film of the format.
- **The riddle:** the classic daylight-saving twins riddle.
  - Ivy is born at 1:55 AM. June is born 25 minutes later.
  - At 2:00 AM the clocks fall back to 1:00 AM, so June's certificate says 1:20 AM.
- **Voice:** Kokoro `am_eric`, speed 1.12. 221 words, 88.9 s.
- **Place:** a hospital cutaway.
  - Lobby: EMERGENCY · MATERNITY.
  - Maternity ward: Room 4 with Mum in bed and two bassinets, plus the nurses' station with the big ward clock and the NOV 2 calendar.
  - Upper wards and nursery, and the stair core.
- **Cast:** Nurse Hana (nurse cap, teal scrubs), Mum, Dad, Grandma, and the twins as newborns and as kids.
- **Top pill:** rolls the TIME (1:48 → 1:54 → 1:55), then "+25 min" for June, so it never shows the 2:00 → 1:00 jump before the reveal.

## Time facts
- In the US (and Canada), daylight saving time ends on the first Sunday in November at 2:00 AM local time, when clocks are set back to 1:00 AM. The hour 1:00–1:59 therefore happens twice.
- Ivy is born at 1:55 (the first 1:55). Five minutes later it is 2:00 daylight time, which becomes 1:00 standard time. Twenty minutes after that, June is born at 1:20.
- So June is 25 minutes younger in real time, and her clock time is 35 minutes earlier: 1:55 − 1:20 = 35 minutes.
- The calendar date: first Sunday in November, shown as "SUN · NOV 2", which matches the 2025 date (Sunday 2 November 2025).
