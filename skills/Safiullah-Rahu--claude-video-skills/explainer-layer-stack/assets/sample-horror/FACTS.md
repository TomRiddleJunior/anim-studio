# FACTS — layer-stack-horror ("What it really takes to run a haunted house")

Checked 2026-10-29.

**This film is fiction and makes no factual claims.** Hollow Pines Haunt, its barn, its scare actors, its guests, the
fire officer and the tall figure are invented. No real attraction, place, company or person is shown or named.

Every number in the film belongs to the story and is presented as the story's event, not as a statistic:

| on screen / in the voice | status |
|---|---|
| "three scare actors", "room four", "forty seconds away", "11 PM", "6:59 PM", "doors 7:00" | story details |
| "the fog sets off the smoke alarm, and three fire trucks join the queue" | a story event at the fictional haunt, phrased as what happened there on its opening night; the film does not claim how often fog sets off detectors |
| "So you add a haze machine, a map of every detector, and a sign-off with the fire officer" | the fictional owner's fix, not safety advice; the film does not claim haze cannot trigger a detector |
| "a safe word … a red light … a hidden door" | the fictional haunt's procedure (safe word shown: PUMPKIN) |
| "Two thousand people show up at seven" / "2,000 in one maze" | story number |
| "Groups of six, every ninety seconds" | the fictional haunt's ticket rule |
| "tickets sold 120 → 164", "in 200 → 210", "people here 0 → 2,000", "IN 11 / OUT 12" | story counters |
| "next guest: 40 s away", "FRI · 11:58 PM", trigger log times "19:02:11" | story details |

Wording note: the voice says "scare actors" (the usual name for performers at haunted attractions) because Kokoro's
"scarers" was heard as "scanners" / "scorer" by Whisper; this is a pronunciation choice, not a claim.

If the film is re-cut with any general safety statement (for example that theatrical fog or haze can activate smoke
detectors), add a primary source here first (claim, value, URL, date checked).
