# FACTS: layer-stack-emotional, "What it really takes to bring a lost dog home"

**Fiction**: Maple and her story, the owner "Robin Ellis", both addresses (14 Alder Ct, 7 Linden Way), the shelters,
"two years later", "forty miles away", the "OLD READER" scanner, the registries "A" to "E" and "Registry C", and the
"universal lookup" window are all invented for the story. Nothing on screen depicts a real product's interface.

**Checked but not used**: the commonly quoted "52.2% of chipped dogs vs 21.9% of all strays returned" and "38.5% vs
1.8% for cats". AVMA's 2024 release repeats them (22% vs more than 52% for dogs; 1 in 50 vs nearly 2 in 5 for cats),
but the paper's full text (avmajournals.avma.org) returned 403, and AVMA's own site sits behind a bot check, so I
could not confirm from the primary source how the comparison groups were defined. The film leaves these numbers out.

Not claimed anywhere: GPS chips, read ranges, prices, chip lifetimes, registry names, how many registries exist, or
any statement about a real company's product.
