# FACT SHEET: "The State of AI — October 2026"

Music video, about 3:30 at **128 BPM** (ESCAPE VELOCITY-style electroclash). Fact sheet as of **18 Oct 2026**.

**How this sheet was built**
- Inputs: the findings JSON from 8 desks (frontier, compute, money, safety, science, work, policy, open-global), plus each desk's verification log (`research/verify_*.md`). The JSON hand-off was cut off partway through the safety desk, so the rest of safety, and all of science, work, policy and open-global, come from the verification logs.
- Only facts marked **confirmed [C]** or **corrected [X]** appear. A corrected fact appears only in its corrected form. Anything unverified is either left out or listed in section 5, "Do not say".
- Source IDs such as (S12) point to section 6. On-screen source lines use the format `SOURCE · PUBLISHER · MON YYYY`.
- **Disclosure:** this film is researched and written with an Anthropic model (Claude). Every Anthropic item is held to the same standard as every other lab's (see section 2).
- **Lyric rule:** each spoken beat is one line of 14 words or fewer, sized to two bars at 128 BPM (2 bars = 3.75 s). The chorus makes no factual claims.

- Navier–Stokes: never say "solved" or "Millennium Prize". It is OpenAI's claim, for the *forced* version, and independent review was not complete as of 18 Sep.
