# THE STATE OF AI · OCTOBER 2026 — video description

A 3-minute music video about where AI stands in mid-October 2026. Every number on screen is sourced, and the facts were checked on 18 October 2026.

**Disclosure.** Researched and written with Claude (Anthropic). Because the film is made with an Anthropic model, Anthropic is held to the same standard as every other lab: no beat praises an Anthropic product, and labs appear in equal type.

**Credits**
- Music: MiniMax-Music3 (open weights; AI-generated vocals and music). The lyrics are original and are a condensed form of the fact sheet.
- Footage: Coverr (Coverr License), Pixabay (Pixabay Content License), NASA (public domain), Internet Archive (public domain) and Mixkit (Mixkit Free License clips only).
- Motion design: a code-rendered explaining layer on an engine after anabology's ESCAPE VELOCITY.

**Corrections made during fact-checking** (the on-screen version is the corrected one)
- The Federal Reserve's October 2026 meeting is **27–28 October**, with the decision on 28 October (federalreserve.gov). Some coverage listed 28–29 October, which were the 2025 dates.
- UK AISI's cyber-testing incident: AISI says it "was not a case of a model escaping". In that test, internet access was enabled and the classifiers were switched off on purpose.
