## Music note
The full mix carries an original 76 BPM D-minor bed made in code (a filtered bass pulse, a pad, a sparse high "data"
arpeggio, a soft kick in the tense stretch), about 20 dB under the voice; it drops out on "On paper". To use an in-app
library track instead, upload `out/odds-rig_nomusic.mp4` and pick a dark, steady suspense instrumental around 76 (or
152) BPM ("dark ambient tension", "documentary suspense", "cinematic pulse") at about −18 to −20 dB under the voice (the
app's music slider around 15–20 %). The ring's sting and ticks are in both files: they are the series' sound, not music.
