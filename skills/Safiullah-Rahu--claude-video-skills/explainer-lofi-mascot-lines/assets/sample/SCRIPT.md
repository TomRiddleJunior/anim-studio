## Retention devices
- The first line at 0.24 s over a finished painted still; one new place every bar (3.43 s); the same calm sentence
  frame ("There is a calm in …") makes the viewer finish each line before it lands; the long line split in two holds
  the middle; the last scene cuts hard into the first, so the reel loops.
