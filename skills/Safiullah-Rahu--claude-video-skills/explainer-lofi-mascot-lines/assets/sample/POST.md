## Music note
The full mix carries an original, code-made lo-fi bed (70 BPM, F major, electric piano, soft drums, vinyl crackle). To
use a platform library track instead, upload `_nomusic.mp4` and search the in-app library for "lofi", "lofi rain" or
"chill piano" around 65–75 BPM, warm and sparse, with no vocals. One bar at 70 BPM is 3.43 s, which is the length of
most scenes, so a track in that tempo lands its chord changes near the cuts. Keep the track around 25–30 % so the voice
stays on top.
