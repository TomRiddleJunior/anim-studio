# FACTS — "Hungary opens the secret police files" (news of 4 Nov 2026)

The story: on 4 November 2026 Hungary put its communist-era state security records online for anyone to search. It is
about an event and institutions (the government, the state security archive); the film names no informer and makes no
allegation about any individual. Checked 9 Nov 2026, five days after the release.

## Sources
- **R** — Reuters (Krisztina Than), Budapest, 4 Nov 2026, "Hungary opens communist-era informer files in belated reckoning with Cold War past" (read via Yahoo News; also syndicated by US News).
- **EN** — Euronews (Rita Konya and MTI), 4 Nov 2026, "Hungary: communist-era files released, up to 60,000 former agents revealed".
- **DNH** — Daily News Hungary (Hetzmann Mercédesz), 4 Nov 2026, "Hungary opens communist-era secret police files – But does a name in the database mean someone was an informer?".
- **PF** — Portfolio.hu (MTI), 4 Nov 2026, "Ma nyílnak az akták: több tízezer ügynök neve kerül nyilvánosságra".
- **OR** — Origo, 4 Nov 2026, "Megnyílnak az ügynökakták".
