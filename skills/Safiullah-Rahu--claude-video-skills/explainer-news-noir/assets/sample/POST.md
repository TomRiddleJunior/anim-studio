## Instagram / TikTok caption
On 4 November Hungary put the files of its communist-era secret police online. About 60,000 names can now be
searched, from registration cards, a 1957–1962 recruitment register and a database once kept on magnetic tape. The
archive warns the records are very incomplete, and a name on a list is not proof that someone informed. The
informers' reports stay closed; more than 9,000 recruitment files are due by 25 February 2027.

Sources: Reuters, Euronews/MTI, Daily News Hungary, Portfolio. Photos: Elekes Andor (CC BY 4.0), Norbert Banhalmi
(CC BY-SA 4.0), via Wikimedia Commons.

## Pinned comment

to the service. Historians ask readers to treat the files with care.
