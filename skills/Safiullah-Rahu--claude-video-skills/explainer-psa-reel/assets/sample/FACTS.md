| 1 | **BE FAST** = Balance, Eyes, Face, Arms, Speech, Time — six warning signs of stroke; sudden loss of balance / dizziness (B), sudden blurred or lost vision (E), face drooping on one side when smiling (F), one arm drifting down when both are raised (A), slurred or strange speech (S), time to call (T) | ✔ | Aroor S, Singh R, Goldstein LB. *BE-FAST (Balance, Eyes, Face, Arm, Speech, Time): Reducing the Proportion of Strokes Missed Using the FAST Mnemonic.* Stroke 2017;48(2):479–481, doi:10.1161/STROKEAHA.116.015169; summary: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FAST_(stroke) |
| 2 | A stroke = blood flow in a brain vessel stops (clot, ischaemic) **or** a vessel bursts (bleed, haemorrhagic) — "khoon rukta hai, ya nas phat-ti hai"; tape "CLOT YA BLEED" | ✔ (general medical knowledge) | NHS / WHO stroke overviews; Stroke Foundation (AU) notes some strokes are caused by ruptured vessels: https://strokefoundation.org.au/About-Stroke/Learn/signs-of-stroke/What-to-do-while-you-wait-for-an-ambulance |

Not claimed on purpose: no survival percentages, no India-specific stroke statistics, no drug names, no advice about aspirin
(the Stroke Foundation explicitly warns against giving aspirin because some strokes are bleeds).

People, brands, names: Dadaji and the family are invented cut-out illustrations; no real person, no logo, no channel
branding. The "Family" chat is a generic green-header chat, not a real app's UI.
