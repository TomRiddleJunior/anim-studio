## Cover note
`out/cover.jpg` is the release frame (gold 101, the shackle open, rays) with the title "THE 101ST TURN" and the line
"100 turns. Not even a click." For grids where the first frame shows instead, frame 0 also works as a still (the figure
at the lock, counter at 0).

## Music note
The full mix carries its own original bed (a low drone that swells with the race, silence on turn 100, a heartbeat under
the reveal, a warm chord on the release), so it can go up as it is. To use a library track instead, upload
`_nomusic.mp4` and add the track in the app (an embedded commercial recording would be muted or claimed). Search the
in-app library for "cinematic piano build", "hopeful ambient swell" or "epic emotional build" around 70-90 BPM, and
start it so the swell or the first big chord lands at 10.0 s (turn 101). Keep it under the sound effects in the race.
