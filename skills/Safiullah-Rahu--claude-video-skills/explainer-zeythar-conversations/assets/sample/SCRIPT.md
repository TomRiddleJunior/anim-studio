**End card:** "What time did you actually fall asleep last night?" with "comment below", and "CONVERSATIONS BEYOND TIME". The loop: the last frame is the clock, and the first frame is "It's 2:30 AM", so a rewatch feels like the same night starting again.

Real-world grounding for the post copy only; nothing in the dialogue claims a study:
- Line 10 is "constructive worry" or scheduled worry time: writing worries and next steps in the early evening, which reduced pre-sleep worry in a trial (Carney & Waters 2006, *Behavioral Sleep Medicine* 4(1):13–28, PMID 16390282).
- Line 15 is what researchers call bedtime procrastination (Kroese et al. 2014, *Frontiers in Psychology* 5:611, PMID 24994989), popularly "revenge bedtime procrastination".
