## What is illustration, not data
- The pallets of cash, the stacks of bricks (one brick = $1 B, drawn to scale: 3 vs 5.5), the receipt's 367 identical lines
  and the departures board are visual metaphors for the numbers above. The banknotes are drawn in code (a generic "100"
  note with a geometric seal): no real currency design is reproduced.
- No Boeing logo is drawn: the ticker card uses a plain "BOEING" wordmark in Inter and the ticker on split-flaps. No real
  people appear. The channel ("Ledger Lane", @ledgerlane) is invented; the CTA is "Follow for the daily market story".
- The phone profile thumbnails ("OIL", "RATES", …) are generic placeholders; no follower counts or other numbers are shown.
