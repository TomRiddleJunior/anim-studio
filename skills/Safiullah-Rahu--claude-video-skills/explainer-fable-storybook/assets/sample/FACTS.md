## The study
**Freedman, J. L., & Fraser, S. C. (1966). Compliance without pressure: The foot-in-the-door technique. *Journal of
Personality and Social Psychology*, 4(2), 195-202. doi:10.1037/h0023552** (PubMed 5969145). Read in full (scanned
copy of the journal pages); Experiment II, pp. 199-201.

## Context (not in the reel)
- Later work found the effect real but usually smaller and dependent on conditions; Burger (1999) reviews the
  mechanisms (self-perception, consistency, commitment and others): Burger, J. M. (1999). The foot-in-the-door
  compliance procedure: A multiple-process analysis and review. *Personality and Social Psychology Review*, 3(4),
  303-325. The reel's wording ("the researchers' guess") keeps the explanation tentative.

## Wording choices
- "safe-driving sign" for "Be a safe driver"; "huge sign on their lawn" for the large, poorly lettered Drive Carefully
  sign; the grids show the real group sizes (25 and 24) and counts (19 and 4).
