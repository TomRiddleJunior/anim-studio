## What was measured
- The engine analyzer (cuts, scene changes and median scene length, motion, holds, palette, brightness,
  colourfulness, loudness, WPM from Whisper).
- 1 fps and 4 fps timestamped sweeps (222 frames) and 18 full-resolution grabs; pixel colours of the label card, its
  underline, the DAY pill, the sign and its scribble, the bubbles, the tag, the photo border, the navy stage and the
  caption states (current / said / coming).
- A stem split of the audio (voice vs music): voice active fraction and level, music level under the voice, voice f0,
  music tempo (79.5 BPM), key (F major) and onset spacing (triplets, 0.25 s), section changes, sound-effect hits.
- Beat timings (label changes, bubbles, the sign, day steps and dips, time-lapse, cross-out, banner, the photo card,
  the navy-stage builds, the return) → the beat map in SKILL.md.

Nothing of the source's script, labels, captions, post caption, characters, artwork or audio is reproduced; beats are
described by their job.
